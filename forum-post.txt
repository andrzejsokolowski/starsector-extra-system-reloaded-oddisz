WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
- Lets you pour credits, materials and story points into the ships you love, until they are as overpowered as you can afford.
- Only your ships. Enemy fleets never get any of it.


Every friendly market with a spaceport gets an Extra Systems bay. Pick a ship, buy upgrade levels, raise its hull quality at shipyards, and slot rare augments with effects of their own.

A from-scratch remake of Extra System Reloaded for 0.98a. The code is fully open source. You don't have to credit me. Claude Code built this anyway.
[color=red]Requires [b]LunaLib[/b].[/color]
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Upgrades[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]13 upgrades with 10 levels each: Durability, Weapons, Targeting, Magazines, Flux Grid, Defenses (shields or phase), Engines, Ship System, Hangars, Logistics, Cargo Holds, Sensors and Conditioning.[/li]
[li]Past level 10, overclock up to 5 more levels for a story point each.[/li]
[li]Hover over any upgrade in the bay to see exactly what the next level does and what it costs.[/li]
[li]Changed your mind? Remove a level for half of what you paid, or undo anything you bought on this visit in full.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Hull quality[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every hull rolls a quality between 80% and 120%. It multiplies every upgrade on that ship.[/li]
[li]Heavy Industry and Orbital Works (and modded shipyards) can raise it, up to 200%. Each step costs more than the last.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Augments[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Ten rare devices, found in salvage or fabricated at Orbital Works: Overload Damper, Nanolattice Regenerator, Cryo-Flux Exchanger, Temporal Capacitor, Phase Coil Tuner, Point Defense Integrator, Hangar Microforge, Missile Autoforge, Vent Thrusters and Fire Control Equalizer.[/li]
[li]1 slot on frigates, 2 on destroyers and cruisers, 3 on capitals. Removing an augment gives it back.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Paying[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Credits, materials from your cargo and storage, or Industrial Evolution ship and relic components if you play with IndEvo.[/li]
[li]Shipyards and your own colonies give discounts.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]In LunaLib's settings menu ([b]Shift+F2[/b] in the campaign): maximum level, overclocking, upgrade strength, hull quality range, augment slots and every price.[/li]
[/list]

[spoiler=FAQ]
[b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b]
Yes.
[b]Can I remove it from a save?[/b]
Not once a ship has been upgraded: upgraded ships carry its hidden hull mod.
[b]Does it work with Exotica Technologies?[/b]
Yes. They do not share anything.
